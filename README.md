<b>ARTICulate is a simple program that automatically opens the azahar emulator (a fork of citra) and connects
to Artic Base on your 2DS or 3DS (provided you started the server, then launched a game). You won't have to
open azahar --> Connect to Artic Base... then type your IP console's IP address everytime, simply just open
ARTICulate.exe and enjoy your 3DS game (just don't forget to start the Artic Base server on your 3DS!).</b>

<b>=====REQUIREMENTS=====</b>

- Windows 10 PC (Windows 11 should also work)
- Azahar emulator downloaded on your PC (https://github.com/azahar-emu/azahar)
- Nintendo 2DS/3DS with custom firmware installed, obviously (https://3ds.hacks.guide)
- Artic Base Server downloaded on you CFW 2DS/3DS (https://db.universal-team.net/3ds/artic-base-server)
- Your Windows PC and 2DS/3DS connected to the same network

<b>=====HOW TO USE IT=====</b>

Simply run "ARTICulate.exe".

On the first launch, you will be asked to select "azahar.exe", and you will need to enter
the IPv4 address of your 2DS/3DS console. Once this is done, these settings will be saved 
to a "config.json" file which will be located in the same place where ARTICulate.exe is.

ARTICulate will then launch azahar, automatically connect to Artic Base on your 3DS, then
you will be able to enjoy your game without the hassle of having to open Azahar and typing 
your console's IP address everytime you want to use it!
